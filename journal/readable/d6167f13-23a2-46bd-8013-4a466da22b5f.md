## Things to fix or clarify
1. **Paper list.** You say "three papers" and "these three questions," but you list six citations, and Golding et al. 2005 appears twice. Map each paper to the level it validates, and name the figure or quantity you'll reproduce. "Replicate the results" is too vague to grade against.
2. **Golding 2005 (and partly Cai 2006).** These papers show *bursty* transcription, which comes from the promoter switching ON and OFF. Your constitutive model will give Poisson mRNA with Fano factor = 1, so it can't reproduce bursting. Either add a two-state promoter or say you'll show the mismatch on purpose.
3. **Elowitz 2002.** Separating intrinsic from extrinsic noise needs two identical reporters in the same cell, plus some source of cell-to-cell variation (for example, randomly drawn β or x). Mention this if you keep the paper.
4. **Label m=2 and p=4 as expected steady-state outputs.** Right now they read like input parameters.
5. **Activator at x=1000** gives ≈0.9999, not exactly 1. State a numerical tolerance for your tests.
6. **r_max is fixed at 1,** so the check "reject r_basal ≥ r_max" only matters if users can change r_max. Decide whether they can.
7. **Add tests for the time-dependent and stochastic parts:**
   - Analytic ODE solution: m(t) = (r/γ_m)(1 − e^(−γ_m·t)) starting from m(0)=0. Compare your solver against it.
   - Gillespie: the mean over many runs should match the ODE steady state. The mRNA Fano factor should be about 1. The protein Fano factor should be about 1 + β/(γ_m + γ_p), which is the Thattai & van Oudenaarden result and a clean link to that paper.
8. **Implementation details are missing:** the language, the ODE method (Euler or RK4, and the time step), the four Gillespie reactions and their propensities, and the output/plot format.
9. **Smaller points:**
   - The ODEs use concentrations, but Gillespie needs molecule counts. Say how you convert between them.
   - The product of the hᵢ assumes TFs act independently. Note that as an assumption.
   - "mRNA transcription regulation, mRNA production" sounds redundant. Maybe call the levels "TF regulation → transcription → translation."
